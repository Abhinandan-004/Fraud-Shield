Scan your screen

Pick a screen, window or tab. FraudShield takes one snapshot, reads the text on it, and checks that text for fraud signs. Useful for screenshots, PDFs and other apps.

    Privacy: the snapshot is read on your computer and is not saved. Only the extracted text (and any links in it) is sent to the FraudShield server you configured in config.js. Sharing stops as soon as the snapshot is taken.
